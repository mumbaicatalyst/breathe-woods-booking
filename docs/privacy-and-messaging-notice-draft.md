# Breathe Woods Privacy & Messaging Notice — Draft

**Status:** Draft for owner and legal review before launch.  
**Effective date:** To be set at launch.

## What we collect

When a guest enquires about or books a stay, Breathe Woods may collect the lead guest’s name, email address, mobile/WhatsApp number, stay dates, party size, selected accommodation, meal plan, experiences and payment/booking status.

## Why we use it

We use this information to check availability, process and manage bookings, arrange meals and experiences, take payment, send essential stay information, answer requests, and keep appropriate booking records.

## Booking messages versus marketing

Essential booking and stay messages are separate from marketing. A guest may receive messages needed to manage their requested or confirmed stay, such as payment, confirmation, arrival and directions information.

Marketing by email or WhatsApp is optional. A guest receives occasional offers and updates only when they actively tick the relevant consent checkbox. Marketing permission is not required to enquire or make a booking.

## Withdrawal and preferences

Guests can withdraw marketing permission at any time through an unsubscribe link in an email, the applicable opt-out instruction in WhatsApp, or by contacting Breathe Woods at `sanil.prashant@gmail.com`. Withdrawal does not affect necessary messages about a booking already made.

## Sharing and payment data

Access to booking information is limited to people and service providers needed to operate Breathe Woods. Breathe Woods does not sell guest personal information. Payment details are processed by the selected payment provider and are not stored in the Breathe Woods booking application.

## Before publishing

The owner must confirm the operating legal entity, privacy contact, record-retention period, final website terms, and any service providers that process guest data. This notice should be reviewed for legal compliance before it goes live.
